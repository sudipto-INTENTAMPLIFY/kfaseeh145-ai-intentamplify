Pricing FAQ

Questions buyers ask before they start.

Why can CPL increase by tier?Higher tiers add evidence depth, account context and buying-group requirements rather than selling the same delivery at a different price.
Does Intent Amplify guarantee SQLs, pipeline or revenue?No. Pricing reflects contracted program scope and evidence depth, not a guarantee of downstream outcomes.
Can we define our own Sales acceptance criteria?Yes. Acceptance and evidence requirements should be agreed during scoping and implemented in the measurement design.
What changes before the Zendesk metrics can be published?The modeled figures must be replaced with CRM-backed values and approved customer usage rights covering name, logo, metric definitions, attribution method, reporting period and exact copy.
